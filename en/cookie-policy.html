What cookies are

Cookies are small files or identifiers that a website or connected service may store in a visitor's browser. Similar technologies may use localStorage and other local browser storage mechanisms.

They may be technically necessary for website operation or used for statistics, analytics, security and understanding how visitors interact with pages.

Technologies that may be used

SovaZone may use technical and analytics cookies as well as similar browser identifiers. Ordinary browsing of public pages does not require creating a SovaZone user account.

  • Technical technologies — may be used for correct site delivery, security and individual website functions.
  • Analytics technologies — help measure traffic, referral sources, viewed pages, device information and interaction with the interface.
  • Third-party services — may set their own cookies or local identifiers under their respective policies.

Google Analytics and Yandex Metrica

The website uses Google Analytics 4 and Yandex Metrica to understand traffic and improve the website. These services may process technical information about a visit, including browser and device data, pages viewed, referral source, approximate regional information, IP address and browser identifiers.

Yandex Metrica may also use cookies and localStorage. Webvisor and click-map features are enabled on the website to analyze how visitors interact with pages. This information is used to improve structure, usability and website quality.

Processing by third-party services is also governed by their own documents: the Google Privacy Policy and the Yandex Privacy Policy.

Managing cookies

Visitors can delete previously stored cookies, restrict their use or block them through browser settings. Blocking certain technologies may affect website statistics or individual site functions.

In some countries and regions, non-essential analytics cookies may require prior visitor consent. Applicable requirements depend on the visitor's jurisdiction and location.

Instructions for deleting and blocking cookies are available in the settings of Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge and other browsers.

Changes and contact

The services and technologies used by the website may change as SovaZone develops. The current version of this policy is published on this page.

If you have a question about cookies or data processing, contact SovaZone through the Contacts page.
