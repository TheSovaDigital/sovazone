How the estimate works

The tool does not pretend there is a single transparent market price. It estimates a realistic range using handle scarcity, length, readability, language, words, names, surnames, geography, digits and patterns.

What affects value most

Clean short handles and strong English words tend to carry the highest demand. Names, surnames and geographic handles are adjusted for international popularity; dots and underscores have very little standalone resale value.

FAQ

Is this a guaranteed sale price?

No. It is an indicative SovaZone valuation range. A real transaction depends on the seller, buyer, timing and specific demand.

Why can two usernames of the same length have very different values?

Meaning, language, global recognition, name popularity, patterns and buyer demand can outweigh length alone.
