Why every Fragment sale is not treated as market truth

Fragment history contains different transaction types. Some cheap purchases primarily reflect converting a username into a collectible NFT, while individual expensive sales can sit far outside normal comparables. A raw average or one headline sale can therefore be misleading.

SovaZone downweights mass price clusters, separates technical purchases from market evidence, and isolates statistical outliers for NFT ownership-history checks.

What matters most for Telegram usernames

Four-character collectible usernames have standalone scarcity value: our cleaned Fragment sample shows a persistent structural level around 5,050 TON. From five characters onward, length alone is not enough; meaning, buyer demand, names or words, language and memorability become much more important.

A meaningless long username can have almost no standalone resale value even if it was once purchased on Fragment to create the collectible NFT.

How the data is used

The source dataset contains 15,626 sales. A large share of cheap or repeated-price transactions is used only as lower-market evidence rather than direct comparable sales. We isolated 128 statistically expensive outliers and checked their NFT transfer histories; transactions with suspicious ownership cycles are not used as trusted price anchors.

The estimate is built in TON first and also displayed in USD using the available current conversion rate. It is an indicative SovaZone range, not a guaranteed future sale price.

FAQ

Why does a cheap Fragment sale not prove a username is worth that amount?

Some low-price purchases primarily create a collectible NFT or happen at a technical market floor. Standalone resale demand for the exact username matters more.

Why is an expensive historical sale not a fixed valuation?

One buyer can pay far above the broader market, and some transactions have unusual ownership histories. The model therefore uses ranges and multiple types of evidence.

Why are four-character usernames treated differently?

In the cleaned dataset, four-character collectible usernames form a separate scarce class with a strong structural market level even when the string has limited dictionary meaning.

Is this a guaranteed sale price?

No. The result is an indicative range. A real transaction depends on the buyer, timing, liquidity and the current TON market.
