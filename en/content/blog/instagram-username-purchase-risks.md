# Instagram Username: Buy Ready-Made or Request a Custom Handle

A short, memorable Instagram handle can become a valuable part of a personal brand or established business. However, buying a ready-made handle and pursuing one exact occupied username are **different types of requests**.

SovaZone works with listed usernames and individually reviewed custom requests, including names that are not ordinarily available. We examine difficult cases before accepting them and discuss prospects, scope, timing, and budget individually.

## Ready-made usernames from the catalogue

If you are open to several names, start with the [SovaZone catalogue](/en/usernames). It features short, rare, and dictionary-style handles for Instagram and other supported platforms.

We confirm the specific offering and its conditions before proceeding. A listed handle should not be confused with a custom project aimed at securing one exact name.

## Requesting a specific occupied Instagram username

A business may have used its name for years while the ideal Instagram handle remains associated with another profile. Sometimes the profile looks inactive; sometimes the situation is more complicated. For the client, replacing a letter or adding a number may not be an acceptable substitute.

SovaZone reviews these cases through [Custom Username](/en/services/custom-username). We look at the circumstances and decide whether the request has sufficient prospects to take further.

**Years of inactivity do not automatically mean a handle can be obtained.** Equally, a username's apparent unavailability through standard search is not a complete professional assessment of a custom request.

## A specific username on an existing account

Some clients want to retain an established account's audience, content, and history while changing its username.

These requirements can also be reviewed individually. The requested name and intended destination account both matter. Details are discussed privately with the client.

## Information needed for an initial review

Include the following when contacting SovaZone:

1. **Exact username** and spelling.
2. **Platform** — Instagram, TikTok, or another service.
3. **Visible status** — occupied, apparently inactive, or unknown.
4. **Purpose** — personal brand, business, public project, or rebrand.
5. **Destination account**, if relevant.
6. **Budget and urgency**, especially for high-value custom requests.

The review helps establish whether there is a sensible path to proceed and what form of engagement may be appropriate. **No universal success percentage or blanket guarantee applies to every username.**

## Security and discretion

Be cautious with anyone offering guaranteed results without examining a request, asking for sign-in codes in unsolicited messages, or proposing questionable actions against other accounts.

SovaZone does not publish sensitive client details or the particulars of individual engagements. Discretion is often essential for public figures and commercially valuable digital identities.

## What if the profile appears abandoned?

A profile that has been inactive for years may justify a closer look, but inactivity alone does not establish feasibility. We review these circumstances case by case and may recommend another name if the original request is not suitable.

Learn more about [inactive account usernames](/en/post/kak-poluchit-nik-esli-akkaunt-zabroshen) and the [Custom Username service](/en/services/custom-username).

## What about a long-standing account block?

Account recovery and username sourcing are separate services. SovaZone also reviews [complex account cases](/en/services/account-help), including prolonged blocks, restrictions, and loss of access.

Unsuccessful standard recovery attempts over several months do not automatically mean that an individual review is pointless. We assess each case independently without promising a universal timeframe or outcome.
