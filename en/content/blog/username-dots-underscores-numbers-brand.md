# Dots, Underscores and Numbers in Usernames: A Brand Naming Guide

When a preferred username is taken, adding punctuation or a number looks like an easy fix. Sometimes it is. But an extra character can turn a simple recommendation into a spelling lesson. The useful question is not merely how short the handle looks, but how reliably someone can find it after hearing it once.

## What an extra character changes

Compare hypothetical examples: `novastudio`, `nova.studio`, `nova_studio`, and `novastudio27`. These are illustrations, not verified available handles. The first is easy to say, the second separates words visually, the third requires naming a symbol, and the fourth may look like an alternative account. Actual performance depends on your audience and brand.

### Dots can improve visual separation

A dot can make two words easier to scan. It can also be omitted in conversation or placed incorrectly. Test it instead of assuming punctuation is always harmful.

### Underscores add a typing instruction

An underscore is a clear visual divider, but often awkward to dictate in a video or over the phone. Ask people to reproduce it without seeing the name.

### Numbers should have a reason

A meaningful year, product line or established name may justify a number. An arbitrary suffix such as `01` or `123` can make a brand feel like a backup profile. The word “official” does not establish platform verification.

## Check each platform separately

Instagram and TikTok have their own username formatting and change restrictions. Confirm the current rules in the platform interface before committing to a name. A display name is not the same as a username, and a missing search result does not prove that a handle can be claimed. Read our guide to [available versus transferable usernames](/en/post/username-available-vs-transferable).

## A four-step naming test

1. **Say it aloud.** Ask three people to write the handle after hearing it once.
2. **Test delayed recall.** A few hours later, ask them to type it from memory.
3. **Check small formats.** Review it on a business card, video caption and mobile screen.
4. **Compare alternatives.** Consider confusion with similar brands and accounts.

This is a practical qualitative exercise, not a universal scoring formula. For a deeper framework, see [how to test a username](/en/post/username-effectiveness-testing).

## When a different name is better

If you constantly need to explain a dot's position, a trailing number or multiple underscores, a new word or cleaner combination may be more effective. This matters particularly for spoken ads, podcasts, signs and word-of-mouth referrals.

Short does not automatically mean memorable. Consider meaning, pronunciation, confusion risk and cross-platform fit. Our [business username checklist](/en/post/instagram-username-for-business-checklist) covers more criteria.

## Choose a handle you can actually use

Shortlist five candidates: your preferred spelling, one meaningful separator version and several independent alternatives. Do not finalize branding until the chosen handle is secured on the intended account and potential naming conflicts are reviewed.
